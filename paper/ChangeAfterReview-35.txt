Reviewer beX1
- Added a controlled leakage experiment that holds selector and
  classifier fixed: top-20 |t| + L2 logistic, SAFE (train-fold |t|) vs
  LEAKED (full-cohort |t|). AUC 0.709 vs 0.903, Delta +0.194.
- The mixed-protocol comparison is now two bars only: leakage-free
  elastic net 0.768 vs frozen FDR 5-edge logistic 0.855 (Delta +0.087).
  The unreproducible dataset-level 0.884 +/- 0.049 was dropped. Table 4
  still reports leaked pooled OOF 0.884 as a different quantity.
- Dataset-level selection is defined as choosing the edge set using all
  146 labels before nested CV; the matched experiment above is the
  fully specified rule (k=20, |t|, same logistic).
- Permutation p=0.005 for the leaked regime was removed. With n=50,
  the minimum empirical p is 1/51 ≈ 0.0196; we report p<0.02 for the
  primary leakage-free protocol only.
- Repeated OOF scores: naive pooled AUC is labelled as such; a
  subject-level AUC (average probability per participant, then one AUC)
  is reported (0.764 vs naive 0.749).
- Fold-wise SDs are described as descriptive, not as CIs, because
  overlapping repeats are dependent. Overlapping SDs among leakage-free
  methods are used to avoid ranking elastic net vs PCA.
- Motion: edges-only elastic net emphasised (0.765); Welch test on FD;
  fold-wise FD residualisation (elastic net 0.770 → 0.694); locked
  70/30 splits (mean AUC 0.733).
- Performance claim scoped to COBRE, Schaefer-100, Pearson FC, and
  classical models — not a general schizophrenia ceiling.

New experiments added at camera-ready (Sect. 2.10 / 3.7, Fig. 8)
- UCLA CNP (OpenNeuro ds000030) is a within-cohort schizophrenia audit under
  fMRIPrep, not a locked transfer. The original 50/122 split is not used.
  Controls were 1:1 nearest-neighbour matched to cases on standardised age,
  sex and mean FD (50/50; mean age 36.5 vs 36.8 y; mean FD 0.255 vs 0.225 mm).
  Matched twenty-edge contrast: 0.679 vs 0.873 (Delta 0.194, same as COBRE).
  Full-connectome leakage-free 0.727. Chance accuracy is 0.50; SAFE accuracy
  0.617. These numbers do not revise the 0.77 COBRE estimate.
- CNP nulls at n=100, 50/50: independent leaked 0.971; synthetic correlated
  leaked 0.873; label permutation of the observed matched connectomes leaked
  0.893. Observed leaked 0.873 does not exceed the permutation floor.
  Script: scripts/run_cnp_balanced.py.
- ABIDE is not reported in the camera-ready manuscript.
- Null control at COBRE dimensions, two feature-dependency assumptions.
  Labels are random, so the true signal is exactly zero.
    * Independent Gaussian features: leaked 0.938 +/- 0.017.
    * Connectomes built from random parcel time series (mean |r| between
      edge pairs 0.10): leaked 0.821 +/- 0.020.
    * Leakage-free returns chance (0.490 to 0.522).
  The leaked COBRE value 0.903 sits about 0.08 above the correlated
  connectome null (0.821) and 0.03 below the independent-feature ceiling
  (0.938).
- Null leaked AUC by k at COBRE dimensions: 0.809 (k=5), 0.937 (k=20),
  0.998 (k=100).
- Optimism vs sample size (null, p=4,950): 0.501 at n=50 falling to 0.263
  at n=600.
- Scripts: scripts/run_ucla_cnp_connectomes.py, scripts/run_cnp_balanced.py,
  scripts/run_null_control.py, scripts/run_null_matched.py,
  scripts/run_null_connectome.py. New CSVs in results/.

Corrections found during a final self-audit against the result CSVs
- Calibration. The camera-ready previously stated that the leaked model
  was more miscalibrated than the honest one. The released CSVs show the
  opposite: the leaked arm of the matched contrast has the lowest ECE
  (0.064) and Brier score (0.136) of any model reported. Sect. 3.5 now
  states this and draws the stronger conclusion that calibration
  diagnostics inherit the leak and cannot detect it. The table row whose
  ECE (0.172) could not be reproduced from the released CSVs has been
  removed.
- "The two aggregation rules differ by roughly 0.015 AUC across every
  regime" was inconsistent with Table 4, where the range is 0.014 to
  0.026. Corrected.
- "Hard thresholding lost roughly seven AUC points" was six (0.768 vs
  0.705). Corrected.
- The twenty-edge screen appears as 0.709 in Sect. 3.3 and 0.686 in
  Sect. 3.4; the text now states that the latter excludes the covariate
  block. The 0.705 of Table 3 (ten repeats) versus 0.709 (three repeats)
  is also now reconciled explicitly.
- Sect. 2.11 claimed the repository reproduces "every number in this
  paper". The unreproducible dataset-level 0.884 +/- 0.049 was dropped
  from the abstract, Fig. 4 and the running text.
- The 0.194 gap is now explicitly the cost of leaking a twenty-edge
  univariate screen, not of leaking the primary elastic net.
- Keyword "Computer-aided diagnosis" replaced with "Validation protocol".
- ABIDE is not mentioned in the camera-ready manuscript.
- UCLA CNP is a 50/50 age/sex/FD-matched within-cohort audit (0.679 vs
  0.873, Delta 0.194) and is not used to revise 0.77.
- Fig. 2 caption states why the left sagittal view has no edges.

What was not added (and why)
- 500+ permutations: not re-run; resolution limit is stated.
- Motion-matched COBRE subsample: not in the GitHub results. UCLA CNP
  was 1:1 matched on age, sex and FD.
- Locked train-on-COBRE, test-on-CNP transfer: not run (COBRE connectome
  cache is not in the public repo). Within-CNP 50/50 nested CV is reported
  (0.679 vs 0.873).
- Nested elastic-net coefficient stability table: not exported in the
  public CSVs; omitted rather than guessed.
- 5x10 repeats for the new sensitivity analyses: GitHub used 5x3;
  this is stated in Methods. Primary Table 3 remains 5x10.
- Reliability curve for the primary model: per-subject out-of-fold
  probabilities were not exported, so ECE and Brier are reported instead
  of a digitised curve.
